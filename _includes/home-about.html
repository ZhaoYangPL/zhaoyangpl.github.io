Wenkai Chen

Undergraduate Researcher · Zhejiang University

I am an undergraduate at Zhejiang University, pursuing a dual degree in Statistics and Information Management & Information Systems. My research interests span reinforcement learning and LLM post-training, optimization of LLM serving and inference, and agent harness optimization, with a focus on context management.

Nowadays, access to cutting-edge AI models is becoming commonplace, but access alone does not make us independent thinkers. AI can help us learn faster and accomplish more, yet it is our own critical thinking and judgment that define who we are. We still need to question assumptions and develop insights of our own. Let us harness AI, rather than be led by it.

Research

  • Adaptive Context Compaction for Long-Horizon LLM Agents — training-free online decision rules for when and whether to compact agent context (Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Zhiyu Zhang).
  • Fragment-Level Molecular Optimization with LLM Post-Training — multi-objective molecular optimization with BRICS fragment edits and multi-turn GRPO (Mila – Quebec AI Institute, advised by Prof. Bang Liu).
