Wenkai Chen

Undergraduate Researcher · Zhejiang University

I am an undergraduate at Zhejiang University, pursuing a dual degree in Statistics and Information Management & Information Systems. My research focuses on LLM post-training and reinforcement learning — in particular agent systems, context and memory management for long-horizon LLM agents, and online optimization for LLM inference and serving.

Research

  • Adaptive Context Compaction for Long-Horizon LLM Agents — training-free online decision rules for when and whether to compact agent context (Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Zhiyu Zhang).
  • Fragment-Level Molecular Optimization with LLM Post-Training — multi-objective molecular optimization with BRICS fragment edits and multi-turn GRPO (Mila – Quebec AI Institute, advised by Prof. Bang Liu).
